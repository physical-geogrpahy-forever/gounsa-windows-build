# 서지정보
Montagnoli, A., Terzaghi, M., Miali, A., Chiatante, D., & Dumroese, R. K. (2023). *Unusual late-fall wildfire in a pre-Alpine Fagus sylvatica forest reduced fine roots in the shallower soil layer and shifted very fine-root growth to deeper soil depth*. Scientific Reports, 13, 6380. DOI: 10.1038/s41598-023-33580-7.

# 이 논문을 찾은 이유
산불 직후 live fine roots와 dead fine roots/necromass가 시간과 깊이에 따라 어떻게 달라지는지 직접 확인하여 postfire root-resistance state를 설계하기 위해 검토했다.

# 연구 유형
- field postfire fine-root monitoring
- sequential soil sampling

# 적용 환경
- pre-Alpine European beech forest
- natural wildfire
- soil depths 0-10, 10-20, 20-30 cm
- root diameter classes

# 핵심 과정
- root mortality
- live fine-root redistribution
- dead root length
- necromass persistence
- postfire recovery

# 핵심 결과
- fire effects were strongest in the shallowest 0-10 cm
- finest roots were most responsive
- fire reduced shallow live fine-root length/biomass
- dead root length and necromass increased immediately after fire
- elevated dead-root signal persisted through the first spring
- dead-root effects diminished with time
- some very fine-root growth shifted deeper after fire

Across the full 0-30 cm profile, burned trees had greater dead-root length and necromass than controls.

# 고운사에 직접 사용할 수 있는 부분
Strong evidence that postfire root state must be dynamic and depth-specific:

```
fire
 -> live root mortality
 -> dead-root/necromass pulse
 -> progressive dead-root disappearance
 -> new-root recovery and depth redistribution
```

The upper 0-10 cm requires special treatment because it shows the strongest fire response and is most relevant to surface erosion.

# 새로운 coupling이 필요한 부분
The paper does not convert necromass into an erosion-resistance coefficient.
That link remains a new coupling.

# 한계
- beech forest
- one wildfire
- root dynamics, not erosion experiment

# 최종 판정
- **채택: postfire live/dead fine-root state dynamics**
