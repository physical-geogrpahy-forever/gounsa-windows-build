# 서지정보
Evans, D. L., Quinton, J. N., Tye, A. M., Rodés, Á., Davies, J. A. C., Mudd, S. M., & Quine, T. A. (2019). *Arable soil formation and erosion: a hillslope-based cosmogenic nuclide study in the United Kingdom*. SOIL, 5, 253-263. DOI: 10.5194/soil-5-253-2019.

# 이 논문을 찾은 이유
고운사가 사암이므로, temperate sandstone에서 실제 soil-production rate의 크기와 soil-thickness dependence를 100년 모델의 지역/암성 제약으로 사용하기 위해 검토했다.

# 연구 유형
- cosmogenic 10Be soil-production measurement
- hillslope catena study
- one arable sandstone hillslope + one coniferous woodland sandstone hillslope

# 적용 환경
- Triassic sandstone
- temperate oceanic climate
- Rufford Forest Farm: arable
- Comer Wood: coniferous woodland

# 핵심 결과
Across both sites:

```
soil formation rate
=
0.026-0.096 mm yr^-1
```

Mean:
- Rufford Forest Farm: `0.048 +/- 0.008 mm yr^-1`
- Comer Wood conifer woodland: `0.070 +/- 0.010 mm yr^-1`

100-year simple equivalents:
- full observed range: about `2.6-9.6 mm / 100 yr`
- woodland mean: about `7 mm / 100 yr`

# soil-thickness effect
Formation was generally faster where soils were thinner.

At Comer Wood, shoulder/backslope positions with thinner soil had soil-formation rates about:
```
0.03 mm yr^-1
```
higher than summit/toeslope positions.

# lithology effect
Both sites are sandstone, but formation rates differed with parent-material character.

The authors interpret the aeolian-derived sandstone at Comer Wood as more susceptible to weathering than the fluvially derived sandstone at Rufford.

This supports:
```
sandstone != one universal production rate
```

# 고운사에 직접 사용할 수 있는 부분
This is one of the strongest analogues currently available because it includes:
- sandstone
- temperate climate
- woodland
- measured soil-production rates

It strongly supports retaining a century-scale soil-production term in Gounsa.

# important caution
Do not transfer the UK values directly.

Gounsa sandstone:
- composition
- matrix content
- cementation
- fracture density
- climate
must be locally constrained.

# current modeling implication
A simple depth-dependent annual production law is supported:

```
P(h) = P0 exp(-h/gamma)
```

or equivalent sandstone-calibrated form.

The full mineral-age PDE of MErSiM is not required simply to represent the first-order century-scale front production.

**핵심 채택: temperate sandstone soil-production magnitude and depth-response constraint.**
