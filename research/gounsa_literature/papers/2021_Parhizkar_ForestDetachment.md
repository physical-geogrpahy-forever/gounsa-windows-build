# 서지정보
Parhizkar, M., Shabanpour, M., Khaledian, M., & Asadi, H. (2021). The evaluation of soil detachment capacity induced by vegetal species based on the comparison between natural and planted forests. *Journal of Hydrology, 595*, 126041. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jhydrol.2021.126041

# 검토 이유
수목 root characteristics가 concentrated-flow detachment와 rill erodibility에 미치는 효과를 산림에서 직접 확인.

# 적용환경
Northern Iran natural/planted forests.
Quercus, Alnus, Zelkova, Carpinus, Parrotia, Pinus species.

# 방법
hydraulic flume, multiple slopes and flow rates.

# 핵심 결과
- natural forest soil의 detachment가 planted forest보다 낮음
- detachment capacity는 root weight density와 음의 관계
- rill erodibility는 tree species/root system에 따라 다름
- unit stream power가 강한 hydraulic predictor

# 고운사 의미
LANDIS-II species/cohort/root biomass를 하나의 generic vegetation factor로 합치면 안 됨.

# 판정
forest-root erosion parameterization/validation source.
full numerical landscape model은 아님.
