# 서지정보
Lei, M., Cui, Y., Ni, J., Zhang, G., Li, Y., Wang, H., Liu, D., Yi, S., Jin, W., & Zhou, L. (2022). *Temporal evolution of the hydromechanical properties of soil-root systems in a forest fire in China*. Science of the Total Environment, 809, 151165. DOI: 10.1016/j.scitotenv.2021.151165.

# 이 논문을 찾은 이유
postfire root deterioration의 1-2 year timescale과 fine-root mechanical decay를 정량화하여 dead-root persistence/decay model의 시간척도를 제약하기 위해 검토했다.

# 연구 유형
- two-year postfire monitoring
- root tensile tests
- saturated direct shear tests
- hydraulic conductivity tests

# 적용 환경
- subalpine conifer forest
- Sichuan, China
- medium- and high-severity fire

# 핵심 과정
- root death and decay
- fine-root tensile-strength loss
- soil-root-system cohesion loss
- root-macropore formation
- hydraulic conductivity evolution

# 핵심 결과
Two years after wildfire in medium/high severity areas:
- root number decreased by about 46-58%
- tensile strength of roots <2 mm decreased by about 36-47%
- saturated soil-root-system shear strength declined by about 55-82%
- decayed-root macropores contributed to a later increase in hydraulic conductivity

# 고운사에 직접 사용할 수 있는 부분
Postfire root protection decays over **years**, not instantaneously.

The mechanical effectiveness per surviving/dead root also changes with time, so a dead-root state cannot be represented only by constant biomass without an age/decay factor.

A conceptual state structure is:

```
DeadRootC(age)
 + DeadRootMechanicalIntegrity(age)
```

or an equivalent decaying effective-root state.

# 새로운 coupling이 필요한 부분
The measured mechanical-strength decay is primarily slope-stability oriented and must not be copied directly into surface-erosion `J`.

It is a time-scale and sensitivity constraint.

# 한계
- subalpine conifer forest in China
- direct surface erosion not measured
- root tensile strength is not identical to erosion-specific binding

# 최종 판정
- **채택: postfire root-decay timescale and mechanical-integrity constraint**
