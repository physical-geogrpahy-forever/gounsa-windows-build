# 이 논문을 찾은 이유
background creep가 하나의 단일 물리과정인지, 아니면 climate and biota가 만드는 여러 얕은 stochastic process의 합인지 종합검토하기 위해 확인했다.

# 연구 유형
- critical literature review

# 핵심 정의
The review describes soil/regolith creep as the cumulative net downslope movement produced by multiple shallow subsurface and near-surface stochastic processes.

Drivers include:
- wetting-drying
- temperature/freeze-thaw
- roots and tree uprooting
- soil fauna
- other biogenic disturbance
- gravity acting on repeated dilation/contraction

# forest significance
The review emphasizes forest ecosystems as especially active environments for biogenic creep because of:
- tree uprooting
- root growth/death
- root-channel infilling
- tree-related soil mounding
- fauna

# 고운사에 직접 사용할 수 있는 부분
It supports defining `q_bg` as a **residual process class**, not a fundamental constant or a single mechanism.

Once Gounsa explicitly models:
- root-growth transport
- tree throw
- dry ravel
- landslide

the residual can still contain:
- wet-dry deformation
- freeze-thaw where relevant
- fauna
- micro-scale granular creep
- unresolved shallow bioturbation

# 한계
- review, not a numerical parameter source
- reported process rates span environments and methods

# 최종 판정
**핵심 synthesis supporting a nonzero but calibrated residual creep term.**
