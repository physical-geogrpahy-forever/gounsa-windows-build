# 서지정보
Lee, J.-H., & Cheong, D.-K. (2015). *Depositional environments of the Cretaceous Gyeongsang Supergroup in Uiseong-gun, Gyeongsangbuk-do*. 2015 Fall Joint Conference of Geological Sciences, Geological Society of Korea, p. 408. Conference abstract.

# 이 논문을 찾은 이유
고운사 sandstone production parameterization을 위해 Uiseong regional sandstone의 petrographic class를 확인하는 사전자료로 검토했다.

# 연구 유형
- conference abstract
- sedimentary petrology
- depositional-environment study

# 적용 지역
- Uiseong-gun, Gyeongsangbuk-do
- Cretaceous Gyeongsang Basin
- lower Hayang Group clastic sedimentary rocks

# 핵심 결과
Regional sandstone samples were classified using Folk/Okada sandstone schemes.

Dominant sandstone types reported:
```
Lithic arkose
Feldspathic wacke
```

Sorting:
- moderate to good

Roundness:
- angular to subrounded

The authors interpret the sandstone as texturally submature to mature but compositionally less mature because of relatively low quartz abundance.

# 고운사에 직접 사용할 수 있는 부분
This suggests the regional sandstone may contain substantial:
- feldspar
- lithic fragments
- matrix

which matters for:
- chemical weathering susceptibility
- matrix/cementation
- permeability
- soil-production rate

This makes a pure quartz-arenite analogue potentially inappropriate.

# critical caution
This is Uiseong regional evidence only.

It is **not proof that the exact Gounsa parent rock is lithic arkose or feldspathic wacke**.

The exact site must be confirmed independently.

# relation to Evans 2021
Evans et al. show that sandstone matrix/cementation can change soil-production rates by several-fold.

Therefore this regional petrographic information is important for choosing the correct sandstone analogue.

# limitation
- conference abstract only
- not a full quantitative weathering study
- not a Gounsa-specific outcrop analysis

# final judgment
**regional lithology context only.**

Do not assign production parameters from this abstract.
