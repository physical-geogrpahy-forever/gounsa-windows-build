사암 산림에서 soil-production function의 고전적 실측 계보를 확인하고, 고운사 sandstone production function의 구조적 근거를 강화하기 위해 검토했다.

# 핵심 결과
Soil production varies strongly with soil thickness.

Oregon Coast Range production function was later summarized approximately as:

```
P(h)
=
P0 exp(-h/gamma)
```

with exposed/thin-soil production of order:
```
P0 ~ 0.268 mm yr^-1
```
in a widely cited synthesis of the Heimsath dataset.

At about 1 m soil thickness, Evans et al. 2021 cite the Coos Bay sandstone production rate as approximately:

```
0.014 mm yr^-1
```

showing strong shielding by thick soil.

# important nuance
The Oregon dataset also contains evidence that very thin soils/exposed bedrock can depart from a simple monotonic exponential relation, with some thin-soil samples showing lower weathering than the peak.

Therefore the exponential law is a useful first-order function, not a universal exact law at H -> 0.

# lithology
The Oregon Coast Range site is sandstone/wacke and forested.

This is more relevant to Gounsa than granite analogues, but:
- Tyee sandstone
- Pacific Northwest climate
- forest/root regime
still differ from Korean Cretaceous sandstone.

# 고운사에 직접 사용할 수 있는 부분
Strong support for:
```
soil thickness
 -> sandstone soil-production rate
```

and for using a soil-production function rather than a constant annual weathering rate.
