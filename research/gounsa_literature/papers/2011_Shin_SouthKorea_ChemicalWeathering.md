# 서지정보
Shin, W.-J., Ryu, J.-S., Park, Y., & Lee, K.-S. (2011). *Chemical weathering and associated CO2 consumption in six major river basins, South Korea*. Geomorphology, 129(3-4), 334-341. DOI: 10.1016/j.geomorph.2011.02.028.

# 이 논문을 찾은 이유
100년 고운사 풍화의 order-of-magnitude를 한국 자료로 제약하기 위해, 국내 하천 유역의 실제 chemical-weathering mass flux를 확인했다.

# 연구 유형
- river-water geochemistry
- isotope and mass-balance study
- regional chemical-weathering flux estimate

# 공간 구조
- six major South Korean river basins
- basin-scale dissolved loads

# 핵심 과정
- silicate weathering
- carbonate weathering
- sulfuric/carbonic acid contributions
- runoff control
- seasonal variation

# 핵심 결과
Average weathering rates reported:
- carbonate by sulfuric acid: about 4.50 t km^-2 yr^-1
- carbonate by carbonic acid: about 11.0 t km^-2 yr^-1
- silicate by sulfuric acid: about 5.77 t km^-2 yr^-1
- silicate by carbonic acid: about 10.8 t km^-2 yr^-1

Chemical weathering explained roughly 66.1-83.3% of dissolved-load inputs after atmospheric and anthropogenic contributions were separated.

Weathering rates were closely related to runoff.

# 100-year thickness-equivalent implication
For a simple order-of-magnitude check, a bulk chemical loss of around:

```
10-30 t km^-2 yr^-1
```

corresponds to only millimetre-scale solid-mass equivalent over 100 years for typical soil/regolith bulk density.

Therefore chemical dissolution may be geomorphically secondary relative to event erosion even though it is biogeochemically important.

# 고운사에 직접 사용할 수 있는 부분
- Korean regional prior for chemical mass-loss magnitude
- runoff-weathering relationship supports LPJ-GUESS hydrologic forcing
- useful sanity check for Hartmann/MErSiM outputs

# 중요한 한계
- large river basins
- mixed lithologies
- anthropogenic and atmospheric corrections
- dissolved export integrates deep and shallow flowpaths
- not a local regolith-front production rate

# 최종 판정
**핵심 한국 order-of-magnitude constraint for chemical mass loss.**

Do not use directly as Gounsa cell-scale front-production rate.
