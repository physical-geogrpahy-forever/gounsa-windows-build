# 서지정보
Marshall, J. A., Roering, J. J., Gavin, D. G., Granger, D. E., Owens, P. R., & Coughlan, J. C. (2014). *Diagenetic variation in the Oregon Coast Range: Implications for rock strength, soil production, hillslope form, and landscape evolution*. Journal of Geophysical Research: Earth Surface, 119, 1395-1417. DOI: 10.1002/2013JF003004.

# 이 논문을 찾은 이유
사암의 soil-production rate를 단순 암종상수로 두지 않고, 교결도와 암석강도 차이가 실제 soil production과 사면형태에 얼마나 영향을 주는지 확인하기 위해 검토했다.

# 연구 유형
- field petrology
- rock mechanics
- lidar geomorphology
- soil-production interpretation

# 적용 환경
- Oregon Coast Range
- Eocene Tyee Formation sandstone
- steep forested hillslopes

# 핵심 결과
Adjacent sandstone watersheds show strong geomorphic differences due to diagenetic variation.

The resistant sandstone beds have approximately:
```
2.5 x greater tensile strength
```
than typical local sandstone.

Low fracture density and higher strength reduce susceptibility to comminution/weathering and alter hillslope form.

The authors infer erosion/soil-production related landscape response to be substantially slower where resistant sandstone beds dominate.

# 중요한 의미
For Gounsa:

```
sandstone
!=
single weathering parameter
```

Critical properties include:
- cementation
- tensile strength
- fracture density
- diagenetic history
- permeability

This is consistent with Evans et al. 2021.

# vegetation relation
The Oregon Coast Range literature emphasizes root activity as an important soil-production mechanism, but current Gounsa production baseline excludes explicit uprooting/tree throw.

Therefore use this paper mainly for:
```
rock strength / fracture state
 -> production susceptibility
```
rather than importing the tree-turnover mechanism.
