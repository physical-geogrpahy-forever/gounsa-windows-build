# 서지정보
Wang, B., Zhang, G.-H., Zhang, X. C., Li, Z.-W., Su, Z.-L., Yi, T., & Shi, Y.-Y. (2014). *Effects of Near Soil Surface Characteristics on Soil Detachment by Overland Flow in a Natural Succession Grassland*. Soil Science Society of America Journal, 78(2), 589-597. DOI: 10.2136/sssaj2013.09.0392.

# 이 논문을 찾은 이유
dead roots와 live roots가 concentrated overland-flow detachment에서 실제로 분리 가능한 보호효과를 갖는지 확인하기 위해 검토했다.

# 연구 유형
- laboratory flow-scouring experiment
- factorial/sequential removal treatment

# 공간 구조
- 1.0 m x 0.1 m test area
- concentrated overland flow
- shear stress approximately 6-13 Pa

# 적용 환경
- 7-year naturally restored grassland
- Loess Plateau

# 핵심 과정
- overland-flow detachment
- dead-root physical binding
- live-root additional effects
- biocrust
- surface litter/stems

# 식생 입력
Treatments were designed sequentially to isolate:
- dead roots
- live roots
- biological soil crust
- litter/stems

# 핵심 결과
The restored grassland reduced soil detachment capacity by about 98.9% relative to bare soil.

Contribution attribution:
- total roots: about 53.7%
- litter/stems: about 30.3%
- biological soil crust: about 14.9%

The experiment demonstrates that dead-root and live-root states can be treated separately rather than deleting root protection at mortality.

# 고운사에 직접 사용할 수 있는 부분
Strong direct process evidence for a two-state postfire root system:
```
LiveRoot
DeadRoot
```

Dead roots retain physical soil-binding effects, while live roots can add biological/rhizosphere effects.

# 새로운 coupling이 필요한 부분
This paper does not provide a universal time-decay function after wildfire.

Using the live/dead split in SWEHR `J` is therefore a new coupling requiring an explicit decay model for dead roots.

# 한계
- grassland, not forest
- Loess soil
- no wildfire
- contribution percentages are treatment-based, not universal process coefficients

# 최종 판정
- **채택: live/dead root separation for concentrated-flow erosion**
- not a direct Gounsa coefficient source
