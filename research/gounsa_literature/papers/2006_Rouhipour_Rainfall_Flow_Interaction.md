# 서지정보
Rouhipour, H., Ghadiri, H., & Rose, C. W. (2006). *Investigation of the interaction between flow-driven and rainfall-driven erosion processes*. Australian Journal of Soil Research, 44(5), 503-514. DOI: 10.1071/SR05006.

# 이 논문을 찾은 이유
rainfall-driven과 flow-driven erosion을 단순히 독립적으로 더해도 되는지, Hairsine-Rose deposited-layer feedback 이외의 process interaction이 존재하는지 확인하기 위해 검토했다.

# 연구 유형
- controlled rainfall/runon flume experiment
- erosion-process interaction study
- standalone landscape model 아님

# 공간 구조
- 5.8 m x 1.0 m tilting flume
- modest slopes selected to minimize rill complications

# 적용 환경
- two contrasting surface soils
- simulated rainfall 100 mm h^-1
- controlled overland flow

# 핵심 과정
- rainfall-driven erosion
- flow-driven erosion
- simultaneous rainfall-flow interaction
- sediment concentration
- deposited-layer/process feedback interpretation

# 식생 입력
없음.

# 핵심 식
The paper defines interaction by comparing:
- sediment concentration from rainfall + flow together
with
- the sum of concentrations generated by rainfall-only and flow-only treatments.

Results show the interaction is not universally additive:
- positive interaction for a silty loam
- negative interaction for a loamy sand

The negative case may relate to rainfall suppressing micro-rills/proto-rills formed under flow alone.

The paper discusses Hairsine-Rose as a framework where rainfall- and flow-driven processes operate simultaneously and interact indirectly through the deposited sediment layer.

# 파라미터와 단위
- rainfall: 100 mm h^-1
- mean drop size: approximately 2.3 mm
- sediment concentration
- stream power
- soil strength and settling characteristics

# 원 논문의 구현 범위
Experimentally demonstrates that total erosion cannot always be represented as a naïve arithmetic sum of independently calibrated rainfall and flow detachment.

# 고운사에 직접 사용할 수 있는 부분
This strengthens the case for an engine such as Hairsine-Rose in which:
```
rainfall detachment
+ flow entrainment
+ deposition
+ deposited-layer shielding
```
are solved simultaneously.

It cautions against constructing completely independent interrill and rill submodels whose outputs are simply added without sediment-layer feedback.

# 새로운 coupling이 필요한 부분
No vegetation coupling is provided.

# 한계
- modest slopes
- no dynamic roots/litter
- flume experiment
- does not yield a universal interaction coefficient

# 최종 판정
- **채택: rainfall-flow interaction evidence**
- supports coupled Hairsine-Rose-style source/sink formulation
- not a final vegetation parameterization
