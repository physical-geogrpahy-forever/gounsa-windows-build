# 서지정보
Deshpande, N. S., Furbish, D. J., Arratia, P. E., & Jerolmack, D. J. (2021). *The perpetual fragility of creeping hillslopes*. Nature Communications, 12, 3909. DOI: 10.1038/s41467-021-23979-z.

# 이 논문을 찾은 이유
all known external disturbance processes are explicitly removed or separated, background creep can physically vanish, or whether granular material can retain intrinsic slow creep below the angle of repose.

# 연구 유형
- laboratory granular experiment
- theoretical interpretation

# 핵심 과정
- sub-repose granular creep
- structural relaxation
- external disturbance rejuvenation
- glassy/fragile soil behavior

# 핵심 result
Even an undisturbed sandpile continued to creep over the observed interval.

Creep progressively slowed as the pile relaxed toward a lower-energy state.

External disturbances can:
- accelerate creep
- rejuvenate the fragile structure
- prevent complete stabilization

# important implication
A hillslope can have a background granular-creep component even without separately named disturbance events.

Thus:
```
explicit tree throw
+ explicit root growth
+ explicit dry ravel
```
does not imply:
```
q_bg = 0
```

# Gounsa use
This is strong mechanistic support for retaining a residual background term.

It does not provide a field-calibrated `D_bg` for Gounsa.

# limitation
- laboratory sandpile
- not cohesive forest soil
- no vegetation/hydrology
- rate cannot be directly transferred to a natural hillslope

# final judgment
**mechanistic support for nonzero residual creep, not a coefficient source.**
