# 서지정보
Lee et al. (2020). Characterizing the Interception Capacity of Floor Litter with Rainfall Simulation Experiments. *Water, 12*(11), 3145.

# 검토 이유
LANDIS-II NECN surface litter mass를 물리적 litter surface area로 변환할 종별 trait 자료가 있는지 확인.

# 수종
Broadleaf:
- Quercus variabilis
- Quercus acutissima
- Sorbus alnifolia

Needleleaf:
- Abies holophylla
- Pinus rigida
- Pinus strobus

# 핵심 물리량
- litter density
- specific surface area, SSA [cm2 g-1]
- surface-area-to-volume ratio

Broadleaf SSA reported range:
- Q. variabilis about 167.83 cm2 g-1
- other broadleaf species up to about 283.88 cm2 g-1

# 핵심 변환
단위일치 조건에서:
```
litter surface area per ground area
=
surface litter dry mass per ground area
*
species-specific SSA
```

# 중요한 제한
이 값은 total litter surface area이지:
- projected cover fraction
- soil-contact fraction
- LSAD
와 동일하지 않다.

overlap, packing, curling, decomposition state를 고려하지 않고 cover=surface area로 두면 안 된다.

# 판정
**NECN surface-litter mass -> physical litter-area state의 유용한 trait source.**
