# 서지정보
Noh, N. J., Son, Y., Jo, W., Yi, K., Park, C. W., & Han, S. (2012). *Preliminary study on estimating fine root growth in a natural Pinus densiflora forest using a minirhizotron technique*. Forest Science and Technology, 8(1), 47-50. DOI: 10.1080/21580103.2012.658233.

# 이 논문을 찾은 이유
고운사의 주요 침엽수형을 제약할 수 있는 성숙 Pinus densiflora 산림의 실제 fine-root length, production, mortality와 깊이분포 자료를 확보하기 위해 검토했다.

# 연구 유형
- mature-forest minirhizotron monitoring

# 적용 환경
- 75-year-old natural Pinus densiflora forest
- Gwangneung Experimental Forest, central Korea
- 410-440 m elevation

# 핵심 과정
- fine-root length
- production
- mortality
- vertical distribution
- seasonal dynamics

# 식생 입력 / 측정
Fine roots:
```
diameter <= 2 mm
```

Depth intervals:
- 0-20 cm
- 20-40 cm
- 40-60 cm

# 핵심 결과
Average observed fine-root length density on the minirhizotron image basis was highest in the upper 0-20 cm:
- 0-20 cm: 2.54 +/- 0.82 mm cm^-2
- 20-40 cm: 0.85 +/- 0.37
- 40-60 cm: 1.26 +/- 0.92

Fine-root production was highest in summer and mortality was highest in autumn.

# 고운사에 직접 사용할 수 있는 부분
This provides a local mature-Pinus constraint that complements the seedling SRL study.

It supports:
- explicit conifer seasonal root dynamics
- substantial surface-root importance
- nonzero deeper fine-root pools

# 새로운 coupling이 필요한 부분
The minirhizotron image metric is not directly the volumetric RLD required by the erosion interface without geometric conversion.

Use as depth/season validation rather than direct parameter insertion.

# 한계
- preliminary study
- small number of minirhizotron tubes
- not postfire
- not direct erosion measurement

# 최종 판정
