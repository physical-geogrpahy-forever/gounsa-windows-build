# 서지정보
Li, X., Niu, J., & Xie, B. (2014). The effect of leaf litter cover on surface runoff and soil erosion in Northern China. *PLoS ONE, 9*, e107789. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0107789

# 검토 이유
Quercus litter mass/cover가 forest hillslope runoff와 erosion에 미치는 효과를 확인하기 위해 검토.

# 적용
- Quercus variabilis litter
- Pinus tabulaeformis litter
- 10% slope
- simulated rainfall
- multiple litter masses and rainfall intensities

# 결과
- litter reduced runoff
- litter strongly reduced sediment yield
- largest reduction occurred from bare to low-moderate litter mass
- additional litter showed diminishing protective effect
- Q. variabilis sediment yield fell strongly as litter mass increased to 1 kg m^-2

# 고운사 의미
NECN surface litter mass는 erosion protection에 실제로 관련된 state다.
그러나 mass-to-protection 관계는 nonlinear/saturating and rainfall-dependent.

# 제한
generic mass multiplier를 바로 production model에 이식하지 않는다.
surface cover and morphology should be independently constrained.
