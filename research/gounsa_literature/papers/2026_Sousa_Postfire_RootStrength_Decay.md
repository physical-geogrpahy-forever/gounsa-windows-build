# 서지정보
Sousa, D. F. M., Fulmer, E., Sanders, M. A., Mathews, N. W., Roering, J. J., Rengers, F. K., Mirus, B. B., Burns, W., Olsen, M. J., & Leshchinsky, B. (2026). *The influence of postfire root strength decay on shallow landslide susceptibility in western Oregon*. Landslides. DOI: 10.1007/s10346-025-02643-3.

# 이 논문을 찾은 이유
최근 다년 postfire root-strength 자료를 이용해 root mechanical legacy의 최저점과 recovery timescale을 제약하기 위해 검토했다.

# 연구 유형
- postfire field sampling
- laboratory tensile testing
- slope-stability analysis

# 적용 환경
- western Oregon conifer forests
- unburned, 4-12 months, 24-35 months, 59-67 months postfire
- 969 roots from 26 test pits

# 핵심 결과
- root strength declined substantially after wildfire
- minimum root strength occurred around four years postfire
- reported overall reduction was about 50%
- projected time to regain about 70% and 90% of prefire strength was approximately 10 and 22 years
- burned root systems showed more brittle failure behaviour

# 고운사에 직접 사용할 수 있는 부분
This provides a recent multi-year constraint showing that root protection can have:
```
legacy decay
 -> delayed minimum
 -> regeneration-driven recovery
```

Thus a postfire root state should not use a single monotonic live-biomass trajectory alone.

# 새로운 coupling이 필요한 부분
The strength metric is for slope stability.
Do not apply the reported percentages directly to SWEHR J.

Use for:
- temporal sensitivity bounds
- validating a dead-root integrity/recovery state

# 한계
- western Oregon conifer systems
- shallow-landslide focus
- not direct soil-detachment testing

# 최종 판정
- **보조근거: multi-year root-strength decay and recovery timing**
