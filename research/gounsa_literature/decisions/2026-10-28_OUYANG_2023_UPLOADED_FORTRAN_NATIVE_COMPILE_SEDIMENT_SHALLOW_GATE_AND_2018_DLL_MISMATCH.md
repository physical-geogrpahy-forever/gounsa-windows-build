- The original *unchanged* `model_deposition_JHE.f90` compiled and was called in a 3×3 synthetic Fortran harness with `-fcheck=all -ffpe-trap=zero,invalid,overflow`. Cases `(h[m], CV[-])=(0.05,0.20)->0`, `(0.15,0.05)->0`, `(0.15,0.20)->4.94478e-4 m/s`. Last result is artificial and NOT field calibrated.
