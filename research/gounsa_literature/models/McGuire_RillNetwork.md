## 2013 core
Topographic update:
```
∂z/∂t = β∇²z + (D-E)/(1-φ)
```

The diffusion term in this paper is storm-time rain-splash/colluvial transport and must not be treated as equivalent to long-term Pelletier-style creep/bioturbation.
