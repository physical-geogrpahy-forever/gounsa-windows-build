## lineage
ANSWERS event distributed model
 -> ANSWERS-2000 continuous model
 -> DANSAT dynamic agricultural non-point-source model

## processes
DANSAT:
- hydrology
- plant growth
- residue decomposition
- dynamic detachment-related soil parameters
- interrill detachment
- rill detachment
- channel detachment
- sediment transport/routing

## vegetation
This is an important lineage because living/dead root and residue states are not reduced to a single cover value; WEPP-derived erosion-resistance adjustments are dynamically updated.

## spatial limitation
Although DANSAT is spatially distributed and has surface/subsurface components, this chat does not classify its overland routing as genuine 2D shallow-water flow.

## 고운사 role
- strong quantitative vegetation -> interrill/rill erodibility precedent
- not a final 2D mountain engine

**vegetation-erodibility precedent, not 2D engine.**
