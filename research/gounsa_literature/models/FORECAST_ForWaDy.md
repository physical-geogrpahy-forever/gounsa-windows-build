# FORECAST Climate + ForWaDy

업데이트: 2026-09-23

## 현재 판정
산림 stand-level 생태계 모델과 일별 다층 수문모델의 동적 linkage 선례이지만, 고운사 strict shortlist에는 들어가지 않는다.

결정적 이유:
- ForWaDy hydrology는 daily
- FORECAST forest growth / biomass dynamics는 annual
- daily water stress가 FORECAST로 전달될 때 annual index로 집계됨
- spatially explicit catchment routing이 아니라 stand hydrology
- natural recruitment is not a fully mechanistic endogenous seed-demography module

## FORECAST 식생
FORECAST는 tree species와 understory plant populations를 함께 표현할 수 있다.

주요 과정:
- annual forest growth
- light competition and vertical light profiles
- nutrient competition
- tree mortality
- fine-root / foliage / wood biomass
- litter and humus pools
- understory populations including grass/shrub complexes in published applications

Plant populations can be initiated from seed or vegetatively, but natural regeneration in several applications is user-prescribed as seedling cohort additions rather than generated by a fully mechanistic dispersal-establishment module.

## ForWaDy
Forest Water Dynamics model:
- daily timestep
- canopy interception and PET
- soil profile with multiple layers
- layer water-holding capacity, wilting point, infiltration
- species-specific transpiration and daily water stress
- canopy, understory and forest floor water demand

## linkage
FORECAST provides annually/seasonally updated vegetation states to ForWaDy, including:
- LAI
- species-specific radiation interception
- fine-root occupancy/biomass
- forest-floor organic matter

ForWaDy calculates daily moisture stress and produces a transpiration deficit index.

The critical temporal limitation is that daily ForWaDy output is aggregated into an **annual moisture/stress index** that modifies FORECAST annual growth.

Thus:
```text
daily hydrology
 -> annual aggregate water-stress signal
 -> annual forest growth
```

not:
```text
daily hydrology
 -> daily biomass/recruitment/mortality update
```

## spatial limitation
ForWaDy is stand-level vertical/multilayer forest hydrology. It does not provide the fine GIS hillslope/catchment lateral routing required for Gounsa.

FORECAST itself is primarily stand-level; landscape tools scale multiple stands/polygons rather than dynamically route water/sediment between them.

## 5조건
| 기준 | 판정 |
|---|---|
| 1 spatial cohort/hybrid | PARTIAL/WEAK: stand populations/cohorts, no actual connected terrain cells |
| 2 explicit understory + succession | PARTIAL: understory species/complexes possible, but mechanistic natural regeneration incomplete |
| 3 soil/root | STRONG locally: fine roots + multilayer daily water |
| 4 catchment/topography | WEAK |
| 5 <=daily vegetation | FAIL: daily hydrology but annual vegetation feedback |

## 고운사에서의 가치
`fast hydrology + slow vegetation`을 연결하는 historical example로는 유용하지만, 폭우 이후 생태상태를 빠르게 갱신하려는 고운사 요구에는 너무 느리다.

따라서 production candidate가 아니라 comparative precedent로 유지한다.
