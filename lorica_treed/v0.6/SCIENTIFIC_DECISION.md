# Scientific decision — v0.6

1. **Keep Hwang et al. (2015) Eq. (5).** The biomass-to-cohesion bridge itself is retained.
2. **Do not treat Korean mean tensile strength as the landslide-event default.** Those values are useful species measurements but are not equivalent to Hwang's near-saturated event strength.
3. **Do not silently choose a reduction factor.** The 1.5–5.1 Wu/direct-shear discrepancy is retained as an uncertainty range.
4. **Do not cap cohesion at 21.6 kPa.** Hwang's 21.6 kPa value is a comparison case and was already too stabilizing in their uniform-cohesion simulation.
5. **Use literature cohesion values only as audit/calibration targets.** For the synthetic v0.5 CI biomass, the P. densiflora 9.0 kPa benchmark implies an effective event-scale tensile strength of roughly 0.8–0.9 MPa. This inferred value must not be described as a measured Korean tensile strength.
6. **No fire module or other unrequested disturbance is added.**
